About This Project

AI-Based Resume Analyzer — A B.Tech CSE (Data Science) Mini Project

Project Overview

A well-prepared resume plays a crucial role in securing employment opportunities. This project was built to make resume review fast, consistent, and useful by applying basic AI and NLP techniques.

Problem Statement

Many students and job seekers find it difficult to identify missing information, organize their resumes effectively, and tailor them to specific job requirements. Manual resume review is time-consuming and often overlooks important details, and most resumes are first filtered by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before a human ever sees them.

Solution

This application automatically extracts and analyzes resume content. It examines key sections — contact information, education, skills, projects, experience, and certifications — identifies what is missing or weak, and compares the resume against a job description to highlight relevant and missing skills.

Technology Used
  • Python & Flask (backend)
  • HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
  • Bootstrap 5 (UI)
  • PyMuPDF (PDF text extraction)
  • python-docx (DOCX text extraction)
  • scikit-learn (TF-IDF & cosine similarity)
  • Regex-based extraction for resume sections
